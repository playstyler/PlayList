    Edit list

    Lines starting with # are sections, (A, B) lists the columns for that section, and * lines are items. Add a definition after ::, e.g. * Kissing :: Kissing on the lips (max 300 characters). Add [safety] after a section name, e.g. #Safety [safety], to answer it with Required / Preferred / Open / N/A. A section ending in a person’s name in brackets, e.g. #Kinks (Alex), or a column with their name belongs to that person. Standard terms already have built-in definitions. Answers to unchanged items are kept. Custom lists, including any definitions you add, are included in share links.

    Save an image

    The image is drawn on this device and saved straight to it. Nothing is uploaded.

    Who’s filling this in?

    Pick what fits you. You can change it any time with the people button at the top.

    Names are optional. They label each person’s columns and the image, and they’re saved in your share link. Private mode keeps them out of the address bar.

    Compare with another household

    Paste the other household’s share link. It is read on this device only: it is never added to this page’s link or history, nothing is saved, and it is forgotten when you close this.
