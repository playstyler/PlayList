# How do you like to play?

Share your favorite playlist to spark conversation and connection with a new couple or share it on your favorite platform! Create a new [play list](https://playstyler.github.io/PlayList/v1.1.0.html) to get started.

## Privacy

PlayList is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser:

- It loads nothing from the internet: no scripts, fonts, trackers or analytics, and its Content Security Policy blocks network requests.
- Your answers are kept only in the part of the link after `#`, which browsers never send to a server. Turn on **Private mode** to keep them in memory only, out of the address bar and browser history.
- The image is drawn and saved on your device. The name is left off unless you type one and tick **Show the name**, and a **Discreet** image shows only section summaries, with no item names.
